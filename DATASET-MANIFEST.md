# MailSonar — Dataset Manifest

## Principle

No real personal, customer or case data is included in this repository, its test suite or its documentation. Every fixture is synthetic, generated inline by the test that uses it, or explicitly supplied by the operator at run time.

## Data handling during tests

- Tests do not contact the internet. Provider, MISP and TAXII behaviour is exercised with local mock servers.
- The ignored inference test starts the bundled engine on loopback and sends one short prompt.
- The browser preview uses the DEV-only mock; it is excluded from `dist`.
- No test writes to the user's real application-data directory except through the app's normal app-data path when the test harness explicitly runs the Tauri app.

## Legacy strings in synthetic fixtures

The generated CSV/JSONL/XML/log/manifest fixtures under `dataset/formats/` were produced before the MailSonar rename and still contain the synthetic subject prefix `Mail DFIR Test Event`. They are immutable test data, not product branding, and were left unchanged so the fixture bytes and their corresponding ground-truth IDs stay stable. Generator scripts and report titles have been updated to MailSonar.

## Not available

- No labelled incident corpus.
- No ground-truth annotations for detection or AI evaluation.
- No real customer mail logs.
- No benchmark dataset at 100k or 1M events.

Any future evaluation dataset must document provenance, consent or licensing, redaction method, label definitions and inter-rater agreement before it is used to support quality claims.
